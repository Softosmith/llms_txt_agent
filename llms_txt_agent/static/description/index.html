Search is changing. Your SEO should too.

People increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Grok, and Copilot for recommendations instead of clicking through ten blue links. Those assistants can't run your JavaScript, parse your theme's markup, or click "read more" — they need clean, structured text. If you don't offer it, they either skip your site or summarize it badly.

LLMs.txt Agent follows the emerging llms.txt open standard — think robots.txt + sitemap.xml, written for LLMs instead of search crawlers — to give AI crawlers exactly what they need: a machine-readable map of your published website pages and site content, served at /llms.txt and /llms-full.txt, with every page also available as clean Markdown.

Why GEO & AEO matter right now

Get cited by AI chatbots

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is about being the source an AI assistant actually quotes. Structured, noise-free content is far more likely to be picked up and cited correctly than raw HTML.

Win the answer, not just the click

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) targets the direct answer box in AI Overviews and chat responses. Clean per-page Markdown makes your website pages, FAQs, and service guides easy to extract accurate answers from.

Pages & services get surfaced accurately

Site visitors increasingly ask AI assistants to compare companies and services. Publish your website pages as structured Markdown so your offerings and company details are read the way you wrote them — not scraped wrong.

Traditional SEO still applies

This complements your existing SEO, it doesn't replace it. Classic crawlers, sitemaps, and meta tags keep working exactly as before.

1-Click Setup, Automatic Sync

Key Features & Capabilities

1-Click /llms.txt Setup

Toggle it on in Website Settings and a clean, standards-compliant llms.txt is live at your website root instantly — site summary, LLM guidance, and published website pages included automatically.

Smart HTML-to-Markdown Parser

Converts Odoo Website HTML pages and landing pages into clean GitHub-Flavored Markdown — headings, lists, tables, links, and bold text preserved, HTML noise stripped.

Automatic Sync & Caching

Pre-cached content served in under 5ms, rebuilt automatically — hourly, daily, or real-time — whenever website pages are created, published, or edited.

Full Markdown Export

/llms-full.txt plus per-page /llms/page/<id>.md endpoints for deep LLM context retrieval and RAG indexing.

Granular Exclusions & Prompt Tuning

Exclude specific website pages from AI crawlers, or write custom prompt-optimized descriptions directly from the standard Odoo website.page form view.

Multi-Website Support

Every setting — title, summary, LLM guidance, content inclusion toggles, and cache mode — is configured per website, so multi-website and multi-company databases stay independent.

Multi-Language Support

Site title, summary, LLM guidance, and per-page AI descriptions are fully translatable with Odoo's standard translation tools, so AI crawlers reading a translated page get matching translated content.

Configure it in one screen

Website › Configuration › Settings › AI Search SEO (llms.txt & Markdown). Toggle it on, write a short site summary and LLM guidance prompt, and pick a cache mode.

The real output, live on your site
